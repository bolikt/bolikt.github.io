About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Inner Mongolia University, advised by Prof. Zhiyi Duan. My research focuses on multimodal intelligence, sequential decision-making, causal learning, and intelligent education. I am broadly interested in developing learning systems that connect perception, reasoning, prediction, and action.
